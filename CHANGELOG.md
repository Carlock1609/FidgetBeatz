# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here, newest first. The version sheet in the app shows the latest entry's bullets as "What's new".

## [0.1.0] — Unreleased

- Soundboard with instant, overlapping playback: pads play on touch, not on release.
- 103 original sounds across 11 categories, all synthesized in-house and free to use (CC0).
- 10 presets: Classic Drums, Hip-Hop, Electronic, Lo-Fi, Synthwave, Piano, Bass, Beatbox, Percussion and Experimental.
- Edit Mode: add, remove, relabel, recolor and rearrange pads (drag or move buttons), with a categorized sound picker and preview.
- Up to five profiles saved in your browser, with recovery from damaged saved data.
- Green LCD sound display with a live level meter.
- Focus mode: just the pads, fullscreen where the browser allows it.
- Keyboard play on desktop (1–0, Q–P, A–F).
- Accessibility pass: stronger text contrast, keyboard pad moves (M + arrows), focus that returns where you were after dialogs, and no zooming pads with reduced motion.
- Footer with visitor stats (offline until a counter service is configured) and this version sheet.
